# Jump Log

Jump twice in the air! And stand on the **Bounce Log** to launch way up into the sky.

- **Double jump**: jump, then press jump again in the air.
- **Bounce Log**: stand on it and you shoot up about 200 blocks. Sneak to stand on it safely. You don't take fall damage after a launch.
- **Recipe**: 1 oak log + 2 slime balls.

Made by Elduin. Fabric, Minecraft 1.21.11 and 26.2.
